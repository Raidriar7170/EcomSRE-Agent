## What the result means

The experiment cleanly separates mechanical constrained-generation reliability
from semantic quality. The alias protocol eliminated the predecessor's full-
object parsing and canonical-order failures, but a reliably assembled generic
answer is still the wrong answer. The next scientific question would be how to
make semantic identity and engineering-gap selection observable without
leaking evaluator truth; this completed study does not authorize that work.
